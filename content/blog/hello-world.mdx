This is the beginning.

I've been building things for a while now, but I've never written publicly about the process. That changes here.

## What I'm building

**Saba** is a proactive AI advisor for relationship intelligence. Instead of waiting for you to search your network, it surfaces non-obvious connections and tells you why now is the right time to reach out. We're in closed beta.

**InfoMonitor** is a narrative topology system. It maps how claims spread across platforms and measures divergence across populations. I built the first version solo in about a week.

## Why write

Writing forces clarity. If I can't explain what I'm building in plain language, I probably don't understand it well enough.
